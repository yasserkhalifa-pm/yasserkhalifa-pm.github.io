How I take a product from idea to iteration

Each step below comes from work I have done, with the source role named.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Frame the problem with research and data before choosing a solution.

    Sprints: found that 60%+ of churners had an activity gap of more than 72 hours around Modules 3–4.

  2. 2

    Define

    Turn client and learning needs into requirements a team can build and test.

    MindShift: problem statements, user stories, business rules, edge cases, and Given/When/Then criteria.

  3. 3

    Prioritize

    Weigh user and business value, urgency, dependencies, and team capacity.

    MindShift and Sprints: RICE and MoSCoW, an Opportunity Solution Tree, and a Top-5 RICE backlog.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    Plan and track work across UI/UX and development.

    MindShift: deadlines, blockers, and dependencies managed in Jira, with stakeholders kept updated.

  5. 5

    Validate

    Check each release against its acceptance criteria before users see it.

    MindShift: Ready for Test reviews of simulation logic, scoring, feedback, and user flows.

  6. 6

    Iterate

    Measure outcomes, test changes, and roll out in phases.

    Sprints: a North Star metric tree, one A/B experiment, and four rollout phases with rollback triggers.
